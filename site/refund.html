Refund policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Studio setup (one-time)

If we cannot complete the installation on your hardware or VPS, you receive a full refund. Once the installation is delivered and working, the fee is non-refundable, but the included support period still applies.

Managed subscription (when available)

Cancel any time; the service continues until the end of the paid period. We refund in full if you cancel within 7 days of your first payment. No partial refunds for unused time after that, except where required by law or in case of a prolonged outage caused by us.

Self-hosted software

The software itself is free; there is nothing to refund.

How to request

Email billing@vividora.app from the address on your invoice. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 10 working days.
