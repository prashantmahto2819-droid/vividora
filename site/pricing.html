Questions

What does "pay only for AI you use" mean?

Transcription, cutting, captions and rendering run on your machine for free. Only the optional language-model features (ideas, scripts, titles) call Gemini or Claude with your own key — typically a few rupees per video.

Do I need a GPU?

No. A multi-core CPU transcribes a 10-minute video in a few minutes. A GPU makes it faster.

Is my footage uploaded anywhere?

Only when you publish it to a platform. Everything else stays on your disk.

Can my team use it?

Yes — admin, editor and viewer roles, with approvals by the admin.
