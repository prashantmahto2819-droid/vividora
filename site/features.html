Features

Every step from recording to results

Vividora is a complete pipeline. Use all of it, or only the parts you need.

Ingest & clean-up

Resumable uploads (phone or PC, up to 20 GB), proxies for instant preview, voice denoise (DeepFilterNet or ffmpeg), multi-clip merge in order.

Transcription

Whisper on your CPU or GPU, word-level timestamps, auto language detection (Hindi / English / Hinglish), editable transcript.

Auto-cut

Pauses, fillers, retakes. Adjustable sensitivity, manual I/O cuts, per-cut transitions, frame-accurate rendering that keeps lips in sync.

Captions

16 presets · karaoke, pop, word-box, Hormozi, neon, typewriter, story · 5 Devanagari-ready fonts · italic, uppercase, spacing, outline, shadow, glow, boxes · safe zones per platform · SRT sidecar for YouTube.

Effects

Auto zoom/punch-in, 16 colour looks + .cube LUTs, flash / dip / zoom-punch / whip / glitch transitions, vignette, grain, cinematic bars, shake, glow, fade.

Overlays & audio

B-roll (full or picture-in-picture), images, logo watermark, animated titles, hook card, SFX markers, background music with side-chain ducking, −14 LUFS loudness.

Shorts factory

Finds the 20–60 s moments that stand alone, titles + hooks (AI or heuristic), face-tracked 9:16 crops, batch render for YouTube Shorts / Reels.

Montage

Beat detection, beat-synced cuts from your clip library, Ken Burns on images, animated text lines, flash on the beat.

Publishing

YouTube (scheduling, thumbnails), Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels and videos. AI-drafted titles, descriptions and tags in your voice. Nothing goes out without approval.

Plan

Trend radar (Google Trends, YouTube top, channels you watch), ideas board, scripts with hook/story/turn/lesson/CTA, swipe file for reference audio and effects.

Analytics & learning

Views, likes, comments per video; which caption style, hook, length, look and posting time perform — and one click to make the winners your defaults.

Team & ops

Roles (admin / editor / viewer), WhatsApp notifications with reply-to-approve, Docker on Linux, Cloudflare Tunnel for secure remote access, archive to external disk.
