About

Made by a creator, for creators

Vividora started on a Hindi motivational channel. Recording was the easy part; cutting pauses, typing captions and resizing for three platforms ate the whole week. So we built the tool we wanted: an editor that does the boring 80% automatically and leaves every decision — and every upload — to the person in front of the camera.

It runs on a home server, costs nothing to keep running, and gets better by watching what actually works on your channel.

Principles

  • You approve. Nothing is published without a yes.
  • Own your stack. No lock-in, no monthly bill, your files on your disk.
  • Style over automation. Tools should amplify a creator's voice, not replace it.
  • Original content only. Vividora is built for people who make their own videos, not for reposting others'.
