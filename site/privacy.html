Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This policy explains what Vividora ("we", "us") collects when you visit this website, request early access, or use a Vividora installation that we host or support. If you self-host Vividora, you are the controller of the data inside it; this policy then applies only to our website and support channels.

1. What we collect

  • Website: standard server logs (IP address, browser, pages visited) kept for up to 30 days for security. We do not run advertising trackers.
  • Contact / early access: the name, email, channel link and message you send us.
  • Managed installations: your account details (name, email, role), the videos, transcripts, captions and settings you create, and OAuth tokens for the platforms you connect. Tokens are stored encrypted at rest and used only to perform the actions you request (upload, read statistics).
  • Optional AI features: when you enable them, transcripts or scripts are sent to the AI provider you configured (Google Gemini or Anthropic Claude) under that provider's terms. Video and audio files are never sent to AI providers.

2. How we use it

To provide and improve the service, respond to you, secure our systems, and comply with law. We do not sell personal data and do not use your content to train models.

3. Third parties

Platform APIs (YouTube Data API, Instagram Graph API, Facebook Graph API) receive the videos and metadata you choose to publish. Use of YouTube features is also governed by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke Vividora's access at any time from your Google security settings or Facebook business integrations; this deletes the stored tokens on our side within 24 hours.

4. Retention & deletion

Early-access enquiries are kept for 12 months. Managed-installation data is kept while your account is active and deleted within 30 days of closure, except where law requires longer. You can request export or deletion at privacy@vividora.app.

5. Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under India's DPDP Act and the EU GDPR) you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port your data and to object to processing. Email privacy@vividora.app; we respond within 30 days.

6. Children

Vividora is not directed at children under 18 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

7. Changes

We will post updates here and change the date above. Material changes to managed installations are announced by email.
