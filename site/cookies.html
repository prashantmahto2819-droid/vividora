Cookie policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This website sets no analytics or advertising cookies. It may use strictly necessary local storage to remember a menu state.

A Vividora installation sets one strictly necessary cookie, vividora_token, to keep you logged in, and uses browser local storage to remember your editor layout. Cloudflare Access, if used, sets its own cookie to maintain your login (see Cloudflare's policy). None of these are used for tracking across sites.

You can clear cookies in your browser settings; you will be logged out.
